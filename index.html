⚠️ Why did Smart Life say "ILLEGAL_PARAM_ERROR"?
The Smart Life app requires a QR code issued and signed directly by Tuya's live Cloud Platform (iot.tuya.com). A code generated offline without Tuya's signature is rejected by the app's security server.

Follow the 1-minute steps below to scan Tuya's official Cloud QR code and link your real devices:
1
Open Tuya Cloud Console
Log into iot.tuya.com → Cloud → Development → open your Cloud Project.
2
Generate Official QR Code
Navigate to Devices → Link Tuya App Account → click Add App Account. Tuya displays the official QR code.
3
Scan with Smart Life App
Open Smart Life or Tuya Smart → tap Me → tap the Scan (⛶) icon in top-right. Scan and tap Confirm.
